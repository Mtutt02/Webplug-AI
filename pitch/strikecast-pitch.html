The insight

You don't need to crack open a Wii

The viral clip paired a phone to a hacked console to steal its motion magic. We skipped the console entirely. Every phone already has the exact sensors a Wiimote has — an accelerometer and gyroscope. StrikeCast reads them straight from the web browser, so there's nothing to jailbreak, nothing to install, and nothing to plug in.

The cast flow

Three taps to the lane

STEP 01

Open on your phone

Tap the link, hit "Allow Motion." Your phone becomes the controller — no app store, no download.

STEP 02

Cast to the big screen

Open the second link on your TV or iPad browser. It pairs to your phone instantly over the web.

STEP 03

Swing & strike

Wind up and swing. Speed, spin and angle all translate to the lane. Hook it for the pocket. Strike!

One game, two screens

The phone feels it.
The TV shows it.

Your phone becomes a tactile controller — a power ring, a spin dial, haptic buzz on release. The big screen renders the full 3D alley: the ball hooks down the maple, pins scatter, the score rack flips. Pass the phone around the room and everybody gets a turn.

Why it hits

Built for the living room

Zero install

No console, no app, no cables. Two web links and a swing. Works on any modern phone + any screen with a browser.

Real motion physics

Speed, release angle and wrist spin feed a true rolling-ball sim. Learn to hook the pocket like the pros.

Pass-and-play

Up to 6 bowlers share one phone. The TV tracks every frame, bonus and the running scoreboard.

Live feel

~20ms phone-to-screen response with haptics and crowd-roar audio. It reacts the instant you let go.

Any big screen

Smart TV, iPad, laptop, projector — if it opens a webpage, it's your lane. Casting is built into the game, not the hardware.

Alleys to unlock
