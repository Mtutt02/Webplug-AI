StrikeCast

Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

StrikeCast is a game you play in your browser. It is built to need as little of your information as possible — which turns out to be none.

What we collect

Nothing. StrikeCast has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers or cookies.

Your phone's motion

When you use your phone as the controller, the game reads your phone's motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) only while you are holding the throw pad, and only to turn your swing into power, aim, and spin. This happens on your device. The raw sensor readings are never uploaded or stored — only the resulting throw (three numbers) is sent to the screen you paired with.

Pairing your phone and the screen

The phone and the TV/iPad find each other through a temporary real-time channel named by the room code shown on screen (powered by Supabase Realtime). Only the room code and the current throw — three numbers describing your swing — pass through it, for as long as you are playing. No account, no name, and no personal information are sent or stored, and the channel closes when you leave the game.

Sound and storage

Game sounds are generated on your device. The app may store a small offline copy of itself in your browser so it opens quickly; that copy stays on your device and contains no personal data.

Children

StrikeCast is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. Questions about privacy? Reach us through webplug.co.
